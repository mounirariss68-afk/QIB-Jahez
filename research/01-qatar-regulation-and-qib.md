## 1. QCB rules on personal finance / consumer loans to individuals

**Origin and date.** The retail lending limits in force today trace to a QCB directive issued in April 2011 (Lexology dates it 10 April 2011). It fixed caps on amount, tenor, instalment-to-salary ratio and pricing, and prohibited the buying and selling of personal loans between banks. Circular number: not given in any reachable source. Sources: https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=d0aed7fc-7335-48b1-a4b6-c48ab5e0f16a ; https://oxfordbusinessgroup.com/reports/qatar/2012-report/economy/all-change-separation-of-islamic-finance-and-credit-restrictions-head-the-recent-adjustments-to-the-system

**Maximum amounts.** Qatari nationals: QAR 2,000,000. Non-Qataris: QAR 400,000. Banks restate these as the cap "including profit/interest", i.e. the cap applies to the total repayable: QIB (https://www.qib.com.qa/en/personal/financing/type/personal-finance), Ahlibank (https://ahlibank.com.qa/en/borrow/personal-loan), Commercial Bank (https://www.cbq.com.qa/en/personal/loans/personal-loan/personal-loan-against-salary-transfer/new-loan), QNB (https://www.qnb.com/sites/qnb/qnbqatar/page/en/enpersonalloansqataris.html , https://www.qnb.com/sites/qnb/qnbqatar/page/en/enpersonalloansexpats.html).

**Maximum tenor.** Qataris 72 months; expatriates 48 months. Sources: Lexology, OBG, and the QIB/Ahlibank/CBQ/QNB pages above. QIB vehicle finance also shows "up to 72 months" (https://www.qib.com.qa/en/personal/financing/type/vehicle-finance).

**Instalment cap (debt burden ratio) and its base.** The 2011 directive set the monthly instalment ceiling at 75% of salary for Qataris and 50% for non-Qataris. Banks state the base as: Qataris "75% of basic salary + social allowance", expatriates "50% of total salary" — identical wording at QIB (https://www.qib.com.qa/en/personal/financing/type/personal-finance) and Ahlibank (https://ahlibank.com.qa/en/borrow/personal-loan), which indicates a regulatory formula rather than bank policy. The same 75%/50% DBR appears in the July 2023 QCB mortgage amendments (https://marhaba.qa/loans-bank-charges-and-interest-rates-in-qatar/ ; https://www.qna.org.qa/en/News-Area/News/2023-07/08/0020-qatar-central-bank-amends-mortgage-regulations). Whether the QCB text itself says "basic salary + social allowance": UNVERIFIED.

**Treatment of existing commitments / standing orders.** No reachable source states the QCB rule on whether standing orders count toward the 75%/50%. Banks require a "liability letter from existing bank + 6 months bank statements" for buy-outs (QIB page above), and the Qatar Credit Bureau gives banks "customers' total exposure in the market and the loans they hold" (https://marhaba.qa/qatar-central-bank/). UNVERIFIED whether non-bank standing orders (rent, school fees) are counted; Gulf-bank practice is to count bureau-reported instalments and card minimums.

**Pricing cap.** OBG: maximum rate "the QCB lending rate plus 1.5 percentage points" (2011). Lexology: "maximum interest rate of 6.5 per cent". Marhaba.qa repeats 6.5% and lists the QCB lending rate as 4.35% (April 2026). Whether the +1.5pp formula still governs: UNVERIFIED.

**Credit cards (2011 directive).** Card limit capped at "double net total salary"; maximum interest 1% per month; cards issued without salary assignment non-renewable at expiry (OBG). Lexis Middle East, 13 April 2011: "Qatar: No Credit Card Without Salary Transfer: QCB" (https://www.lexismiddleeast.com/news/2011-04-13_28/en).

**Salary transfer.** No reachable QCB text mandates salary transfer for personal loans, but every Qatari bank product page requires salary assignment to the lending bank: Ahlibank, CBQ ("Salary Transfer Letter"), Dukhan Bank (https://www.dukhanbank.com/useful-tools/faqs), QIB ("salary credited to QIB account" for Instant Finance, https://www.qib.com.qa/en/personal/financing/benefits/financing-benefits/instant-finance). For expatriates, banks also take an end-of-service assignment and post-dated security cheques (Ahlibank). Marhaba.qa: "No post-dated cheques permitted for loan collateral" for Qataris.

**Age limits.** No QCB text reachable. QIB: Qataris 18–65, residents 21–60 (https://www.qib.com.qa/en/personal/financing/type/personal-finance); Ahlibank: Qataris 18 at application / 70 at maturity, expatriates 21 / 60 at maturity. The expatriate 60-at-maturity figure is consistent across banks; whether it is a QCB rule: UNVERIFIED.

**2019–2026 amendments.** Only the July 2023 real-estate finance amendment was found. In April 2016 QCB publicly denied reports of tightened personal-loan rules (https://www.gulf-times.com/story/487658/Central-bank-denies-personal-loan-curbs). No evidence of a change to the QAR 2m/400k, 72/48 month or 75%/50% parameters since 2011.

## 2. QCB customer protection / conduct rules

QCB has a Customer Protection Department (https://www.qcb.gov.qa/en/pages/customer-protection-department.aspx ; content not retrievable). QCB can fine banks up to QAR 10 million for breaches; a March 2014 directive required banks to answer customer-care calls and resolve complaints promptly, no day-count given (https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/02/03/2014/qcb-warns-banks-on-customers-complaints). Complaint time limits, notice periods, Arabic-language requirement, collection-conduct rules, contact hours: UNVERIFIED (no QCB text reachable). Bank tariffs state charges are "subject to change without prior notice, provided that the Bank shall comply with Qatar Central Bank's instructions" (https://www.cbq.com.qa/-/media/project/cbq/cbqwebsite/documents/tarrif-of-charges/cbq-personal-banking-tarrif-of-charges-en.pdf). A debt-collection vendor blog claims QCB guidelines prohibit harassment and inconvenient-hours contact but is unsourced (https://www.debt-works.com/consumer-debt-collection-in-qatar-best-practices-and-legal-considerations/) — UNVERIFIED.

**QCB Data Handling and Protection Regulation (February 2025).** Issued 16–17 February 2025, applies to QCB-licensed institutions and fintechs; covers data governance, customer protection, data security, business continuity. Vendor summaries describe explicit consent before data is collected, processed, shared or retained (except where legally/contractually required), separate consent for marketing, purpose limitation, a retention policy with deletion rights, and third-party checks before sharing. Sources: https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/17/02/2025/qcb-issues-data-handling-and-protection-regulation ; https://www.mondaq.com/financial-services/1590298/the-qatar-central-bank-issues-data-handling-and-protection-regulations ; https://securiti.ai/solutions/qatar-central-bank-data-handling-and-protection-regulation/ . Primary PDF not reachable; specifics UNVERIFIED against the text.

## 3. Personal data law (Law 13 of 2016), NCGAA guidance, and CRA rules on unsolicited messages

**Law No. 13 of 2016 (PDPPL).** Art. 22: electronic communications for direct marketing are forbidden "except after obtaining the prior consent" of the individual; the message must identify the sender, state that it is marketing, and give an easily reachable address to request cessation. Art. 10: no retention beyond the necessary period; accuracy and relevance. Art. 5: consent withdrawable at any time. Art. 16: data "of a special nature" needs permission from the competent department. Arts. 23–24: fines up to QAR 1 million (Arts. 4, 8–12, 14–15, 22) and QAR 5 million (Arts. 13, 16, 17). English text: https://www.dataguidance.com/sites/default/files/law_no._13_of_2016_on_protecting_personal_data_privacy_-_english.pdf ; corroborated by https://www.sharqlawfirm.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Article-Data-Protection.pdf and https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?t=electronic-marketing&c=QA .

**Regulator.** Originally the Compliance and Data Protection Department of MoTC (guidelines December 2020); now the National Cyber Governance and Assurance Affairs (NCGAA) under the National Cyber Security Agency. Breach notification within 72 hours (InCountry summary; article reference UNVERIFIED). https://incountry.com/blog/qatar-data-protection-law-guide-for-global-companies/

**NCSA guideline "Electronic Communications for Direct Marketing" (PDPPL-02050218E, Nov 2020, updated Sept 2022)** exists (https://ncsa.gov.qa/shared/document_library/94787172324409/33000/8860/1.0~41288b9d-9fd0-474b-9e8c-7eca3eedf743) but its content could not be read. UNVERIFIED beyond existence.

**ID copies.** No Qatar-specific rule on retention of QID copies beyond Art. 10's necessity principle and the 10-year AML record-keeping obligation. AML retention is the governing floor for KYC documents.

**CRA Spam Regulation (amended 6 August 2017).** Art. 6(2)(c): no direct marketing without prior consent. Art. 6(2)(d): "No Direct Marketing shall be sent between the hours of 21:00 and 09:00." Art. 6(3): withdrawal of consent effective within two business days; SMS/MMS must carry a free, functional unsubscribe. Art. 6(4): sender identification in Arabic and English, valid for at least 30 days. Consent presumed where an existing relationship exists and the message is relevant to it, with easy opt-out (e-Commerce Law Art. 54(b)). Art. 8(e): breaches may be referred to the Public Prosecutor. Source: https://www.cra.gov.qa/-/media/System/1/A/1/B/1A1B31855B7654C4A360A3C20E55D739/Spam-Regulation-Amended-English-Version.ashx . Vendor guide (8am–9pm, OTP exempt): https://www.sent.dm/en/resources/sms-compliance/qatar-sms-guide ; the CRA text is the authority.

Design implication: a remediation message ("your QID has expired, update it to keep your offer") is a service message tied to an existing relationship, but it must carry sender identity and opt-out and should respect the 09:00–21:00 window.

## 4. Qatar Credit Bureau and bounced cheques

**Bureau.** Established under QCB Decision No. 5 of 2008; operational since 2011 (https://marhaba.qa/qatar-central-bank/ ; https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/25/03/2021/Qatar-Credit-Bureau-launches-new-service-providing-credit-reports-online). Members: banks, financial institutions, finance companies; Ooredoo and Vodafone Qatar joined February 2016. About 370,000 reports a year to members (322,000 on individuals). Operates under QCB (https://www.cb.gov.qa/English/Services/Pages/becomemember.aspx).

**What it reports.** Credit facilities from any provider, a summary of overall credit standing, dishonoured cheques (https://www.cb.gov.qa/English/Services/Pages/ourservices.aspx). It cannot grant credit or impose restrictions on banks (marhaba.qa).

**Credit score.** A "Credit Scoring" product exists (https://www.cb.gov.qa/English/Pages/default.aspx). Score range and bands: UNVERIFIED.

**Consent.** "No entity can access your credit report without your prior consent." Consent via SMS reply, the bureau app, or the e-services portal, behind the National Authentication System (https://www.cb.gov.qa/English/Services/Pages/Electronic-Consent-Service.aspx). Implication: a pre-approval engine needs a standing or in-journey consent step for bureau inquiries.

**Retention — dishonoured cheques.** Kept "for a period of three years from the date of the cheque settlement"; no new cheque books until dishonoured amounts are paid; status updates to "paid" once settled (https://www.cb.gov.qa/English/Services/Pages/Bouncedchequereport.aspx). Retention for loan defaults: UNVERIFIED.

**Bounced cheques — criminal position.** Penal Code (Law 11 of 2004) Art. 357: imprisonment three months to three years and/or fine QAR 3,000–10,000 (https://www.raya.com/2026/09/03/تعزيز-الوعي-القانوني-يقلل-الشيكات-المرتجعة ; https://www.business.hsbc.com.qa/-/media/library/markets-selective/qatar/pdf/download-centre/qatar-account-disclosures-english.pdf ; https://www.gulf-times.com/story/413572/penalties-for-a-bounced-cheque). Art. 358: penalty may be dropped or suspended on settlement at any stage. 2019: cheque-book withdrawal and a blacklist of habitual offenders; 37,130 cheque cases filed in 2018 (https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/05/08/2019/New-procedures-to-deter-bounced-cheques).

**2022–2026 changes.** Law No. 1 of 2024 (effective 6 April 2024) replaced Art. 585 of the Commercial Law: mandatory partial payment of the available balance, annotation, certificate of partial payment; criminal liability unchanged (https://qatarlaw.com/article/a-new-commercial-law-amendment-regarding-cheques ; https://www.tasshamjit.com/qatar-introduces-cheque-payment-reforms-key-amendments-to-commercial-law/). Al Raya (Sept 2026): QCB measures include transaction-history review before issuing cheque books, a central returned-cheque database, listing in credit reports, no new cheque books until full payment, QAR 500 administrative fee per returned cheque; beneficiaries have 30 days to file a criminal complaint. HSBC Qatar's disclosures confirm listing in the bounced-cheques report and possible account closure for repeat returns.

## 5. "Cautionary note" / caution flags in Gulf core banking systems

No Qatar-specific public taxonomy found. UNVERIFIED as a QCB category list. Supporting sources:

- Core systems implement these as posting restrictions or fund blocks set by the bank or third parties (courts, regulators); Temenos names death, creditor judgments, payment/clearing holds and disputes (https://developer.temenos.com/article/end-end-account-services-workflow).
- Court attachment/freeze under Judicial Enforcement Law No. 4 of 2024: electronic identification of bank assets (Art. 100); freezing orders (Art. 102); salary attachment capped at 25% of net monthly income (Art. 57); travel bans (Art. 36); automatic cancellation on full satisfaction with a discharge certificate (Art. 37) (https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/enforcement-of-judgments-2026/qatar).
- Sanctions: Public Prosecutor Decision No. 1 of 2020 — freeze within 12 hours (domestic) / 24 hours (UN), release within three business days of delisting (https://www.sc.com/qa/uploads/sites/39/content/docs/Decision-No.-1-of-2020-Regulating-the-Implementation-Mechanisms-EN.pdf). QIB screens against Qatar, UN, UK HMT, EU and US lists (https://www.qib.com.qa/en/sustainability/frameworks-and-policies/amlcft-position-statement).
- Deceased: CBQ terms close the current account on death, incapacity, bankruptcy, insolvency or arrest (Clause 4(f)) (https://www.cbq.com.qa/-/media/project/cbq/cbqwebsite/documents/terms-and-conditions/cb-retail-terms-and-conditions-en.pdf).
- Employment ended / salary stopped: CBQ documents a "Blocked" status triggered by an employer's termination notification or end-of-service payment and by an outstanding monthly payment; recovery requires a new QID, new employment details with salary certificate, and receipt of the first salary (https://www.cbq.com.qa/en/personal/ways-to-bank/account-recovery-guide). QNB's loan terms make the loan immediately due on change of employer, resignation or termination (https://www.qnb.com/sites/qnb/qnbqatar/document/en/PLTC).
- KYC-expired: Gulf Bank (Kuwait) terms allow stopping transactions if the customer refuses regulatory documents, reactivating when the reason ends (https://www.e-gulfbank.com/media/1451d28cef56e38e649ce0eee6b75201.pdf).

Working taxonomy (UNVERIFIED as QIB's codes): deceased; court attachment/freeze; sanctions/designation; fraud/AML investigation; legal hold; KYC/QID expired; salary stopped / employer termination notice; delinquency block; dormant. Only KYC-expired, salary-stopped, delinquency and dormant are customer-remediable.

November 2024: AI-based credit risk models "enable pre-approval for the award-winning One Click finance and instant credit card offerings"; the app has "over 280 features" (https://www.qib.com.qa/en/about-us/media-center/news/news-listing/2024/11/03/qib-expands-international-wallet-transfers-and-enhances-digital-aipowered-features-on-its-mobile-app). Instant Finance eligibility: salary credited to a QIB account; fixed-deposit holders (up to 100% against the deposit); existing finance customers for top-ups; four steps, no paperwork, app only (https://www.qib.com.qa/en/personal/financing/benefits/financing-benefits/instant-finance).

**Digital share (2023).** 52% of retail sales through digital channels; 71% of new accounts opened digitally; 83% of retail customers digitally active (https://www.theasianbanker.com/updates-and-articles/qatar-islamic-bank-redefines-retail-banking-in-qatar-through-digital-innovation-customer-focus-and-sustainable-growth-2024).

**Personal Finance product.** Qataris 18–65, residents 21–60; confirmed employment with a QIB-approved employer; instalment ≤ 75% of basic salary + social allowance (Qataris) or 50% of total salary (residents); minimum QAR 15,000; maximum QAR 2m / QAR 400k "including profit rate"; documents: salary certificate, valid QID (Qataris) or residency permit + passport (expats), liability letter + 6 months statements for buy-outs; same-day financing, grace period up to 3 months, Credit Shield Takaful, top-up, no processing fees (https://www.qib.com.qa/en/personal/financing/type/personal-finance). Shari'a structure not named on the page: UNVERIFIED.

**Vehicle Finance.** Same age bands and DBR formula; Qataris no down payment, expats 20% minimum; tenor up to 72 months (https://www.qib.com.qa/en/personal/financing/type/vehicle-finance). Other: Home Finance up to 240 months, Qard Hasan for retirees up to QAR 300,000.

**Salary and employer conditions.** "Your employer must be a company listed with QIB for you to be eligible for special finance rates" (https://www.qib.com.qa/en/help-support/personal/accounts/new-to-qatar). "Your employer needs to be registered with QIB for salary transfers"; if not, QIB coordinates with HR/payroll (https://www.qib.com.qa/en/help-support/personal/accounts/switch-to-qib). QIB@work targets employees of government, semi-government or large private companies earning over QAR 5,000 (https://www.qib.com.qa/en/personal/special-programs/qibwork/). Minimum salary for personal finance not stated: UNVERIFIED.

**Segments (names on qib.com.qa).** Private Banking (qualifying total relationship value not disclosed; by invitation/referral; centre at Al Sadd) (https://www.qib.com.qa/en/personal/accounts/tiers/private-banking). Tamayuz Banking (QAR 350,000 average deposit for 6 months, or salary credit QAR 50,000/month, or non-salaried with financing ≥ QAR 3m and inflows ≥ QAR 1.5m/yr; dedicated RM and call centre) (https://www.qib.com.qa/en/personal/accounts/tiers/tamayuz-banking). **Channels.** Mobile app (iOS, Android, Huawei), Internet Banking, SMS Banking, Zaki AI virtual assistant (website and app, Arabic/English; can update personal details and request callbacks) (https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/07/11/2021/qib-introduces-new-features-to-its-ai-virtual-assistant-zaki), 24-hour phone lines, email info@qib.com.qa, web contact form with preferred contact time (https://www.qib.com.qa/en/about-us/contact-us). ## 7. QID / passport expiry and bank records

**QID validity.** Qatari citizens: 10 years (5 for under-16s) (https://www.wikiprocedure.com/index.php/Qatar_-_Renew_Identity_(ID)_Card). Expatriate residence permits: MOI renews for "1 to 3 years" (https://hukoomi.gov.qa/en/services/renew-residence-permit-for-expatriates); 5-year categories reported by unofficial guides — UNVERIFIED.

**Grace and fines.** Law 21 of 2015 Art. 10(1): RP procedures within 30 days of entry; fine QAR 10/day capped at QAR 6,000 by reconciliation, up to QAR 10,000 judicially (https://thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/08/07/2024/fine-up-to-qr10000-for-not-completing-residence-permit-procedures-reminds-moi). A 90-day post-expiry grace at QAR 10/day is stated by unofficial guides — UNVERIFIED.

**Bank obligation to hold a valid QID.** QNB: QID within 90 days of opening "otherwise the account will be blocked as per Qatar Central Bank policy" (https://www.qnb.com/sites/qnb/qnbqatar/page/en/encurrentaccountpage.html). Doha Bank: "If you do not update your information, some of your Banking services might be impacted" (https://www.dohabank.com.qa/customer-information-update/). CBQ: customers must inform the bank "immediately" of changes in legal, residential or employment status (Clause 23(b)). QNB loan terms: borrower must keep mobile and email current.

**AML law basis.** Law No. 20 of 2019: CDD on establishing a relationship, transactions ≥ QAR 50,000, suspicion, and "when doubts arise regarding previously collected customer data"; ongoing due diligence; records ≥ 10 years (https://www.addleshawgoddard.com/en/insights/insights-briefings/2020/corporate-crime--investigations-update/qatars-new-anti-money-laundering-law/ ; https://www.getfocal.ai/blog/anti-money-laundering-laws-in-qatar). QFCRA guidance: firms "should obtain updated CDD information (including updated copies of the customer's passport or identity documents if these have expired)"; review frequency follows risk rating (https://eforms.qfcra.com/PublishedFiles/AttachmentsToNotice/Notice_50/Guidance%20on%20Customer%20Due%20Diligence%20March%202020.pdf). QCB's own fixed refresh cycle: UNVERIFIED.

**Standard documents banks request.** Salary certificate; valid QID; passport copy (expats); residence permit; salary transfer letter; end-of-service assignment and post-dated security cheques (expats); liability letter + 6 months statements for buy-outs; showroom quotation for vehicle finance.

## 8. Dormant and "status not normal" accounts

**Dormancy trigger.** HSBC Qatar's QCB-governed disclosures: dormant after "one year for current accounts, and five years for term and call deposit accounts" with no transactions and no contact (HSBC PDF above). QCB instruction text: UNVERIFIED. Dormant-account fees uniform at QAR 10/month on balances under QAR 500 (CBQ, Dukhan, Ahlibank tariffs), suggesting a QCB fee cap (QCB document 13-141 exists, not reachable).

**Non-normal statuses documented.** Blocked for employer termination notice / EOS payment; blocked for outstanding instalment (CBQ recovery guide). Closed on death, incapacity, bankruptcy, insolvency or arrest (CBQ T&Cs). Blocked for failure to present QID within 90 days (QNB) or to refresh KYC (QIB). Court attachment/freeze (JEL 2024). Sanctions freeze (Decision 1/2020). A QCB-defined enumeration: UNVERIFIED.

## Ten most load-bearing facts and confidence

1. QCB retail caps: QAR 2m / 72 months (Qataris), QAR 400k / 48 months (expatriates), including profit. High.
2. Instalment cap: 75% of basic salary + social allowance (Qataris); 50% of total salary (expatriates). High for percentages; medium-high for the base wording.
3. Credit Bureau reports need the customer's prior consent (SMS/app/portal); dishonoured-cheque records stay three years from settlement. High.
4. Bounced cheques remain criminal (Art. 357); Law 1/2024 mandatory partial payment; Art. 358 lapse on settlement. High.
5. Direct electronic marketing needs prior consent (PDPPL Art. 22; CRA Art. 6(2)(c)); none 21:00–09:00; opt-out within two business days; existing-relationship messages presumed consented with opt-out. High.
6. Expired/missing QID leads to block/closure (QNB 90 days; QIB closure warning); AML Law 20/2019 CDD refresh and 10-year retention. High for bank practice; medium for the QCB instruction.
7. Dormancy: one year (current), five years (term); QAR 10/month fee under QAR 500. Medium.
8. QIB One Click/Instant Finance: AI pre-approval; eligibility = salary credited to QIB, or deposit holder, or existing finance customer; SMS/app notification; funds in minutes. High.
9. QIB segments: Private Banking, Tamayuz (QAR 50k salary or QAR 350k deposits), Essential Banking. High.
10. Court freezes lift on satisfaction with discharge certificate; salary attachment capped at 25%; sanctions freezes lift only on delisting. High.

To obtain directly from QCB documents: Instructions to Banks consumer-credit chapter (age at maturity, standing orders, salary transfer); Credit Bureau chapter (default-record retention, mandatory inquiry); the 2025 Data Handling and Protection Regulation; Customer Protection complaint time limits; fee-cap circular 13-141.
