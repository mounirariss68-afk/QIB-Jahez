The budget (SCREEN-DISCIPLINE rule 6): a screen has one hero and at most three panels before the fold; a decision card has one headline, up to four facts, one recommendation line, one or two actions and one disclosure; a list row is one line and one status. Behind one click (rule 4): steps, decision trail, rules and clauses cited, agent rungs, system calls, internal ids, governance copy.
